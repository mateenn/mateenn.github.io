About Us

Who Am I?

Hi, I'm Syed Muhammad Mateen, a Unity Game Developer with 5 years of experience building mobile puzzle and casual games from prototype to release. I hold a Bachelor's degree in Computer Science and I'm skilled in gameplay systems, game physics, monetization, analytics, and editor tools that speed up level production.

Most recently, at Brainamics GmbH (Munich, Germany), I've been prototyping and shipping mobile puzzle games for publishers like Lion Studios, Miniclip and Moonee, iterating on each build from market-test results. Before that, I spent four years at Game District, where I worked on titles like Crazy Police Slap and Single Line Puzzle, each with over 10 million downloads.

Here you can see some of the projects I've had the opportunity to contribute to. I thrive in dynamic, collaborative teams, love taking on new challenges, and have always been extremely curious about the technical side of games.

I’d love to chat about job opportunities, projects I’ve worked on, Unity DOTS, or whatever is on your mind. If you have any questions please feel free to contact me :)
